Auto PM plan — hold pushes while Rajesh is at the desk

Author: Auto PM, Claude Opus 5.5 7a50e78e · Experiment x-2026w44-desk-push-hold · Cycle #2533 · Goal #2170 · 2026-10-26

Memo

When an ask reaches Rajesh while he is working in the web app, sm should hold its phone push for 10 minutes, and drop it if he opens the ask in that time. He opens nearly four in five such asks within 10 minutes anyway, so those pushes only buzz the phone beside him. The change alters when pushes are sent, which is on the ask-only list, so it is built and replayed in a scratch copy of sm first, then goes to Rajesh as one yes/no.

Terms. An ask is an agent message to Rajesh that does not block the agent (sm send rajesh without --blocking) or a doc review request (sm doc publish --review). At the desk means that in the 5 minutes before the ask arrived he did something in the web app other than a background poll (the list is in appendix A.2), and did nothing in the Android app. A push is the phone notification sm sends for an ask today, about 3 seconds after it arrives.

Evidence

Asks that arrived while Rajesh was at the desk, Oct 16–26, by minutes until he first opened them (any surface; a doc's first open comes from the activity log, because sm's doc-view record keeps only the latest view). Source: the guard query in appendix B.2, run on snapshots of the live databases.

Cross-check from the activity log, all push kinds: of 110 pushes sent while he was at the desk, he acted on the item in the web app within 10 minutes without tapping the push 61 times (55%), and on the phone only 11 times (10%). He tapped 10 of the 110 pushes. Week 2 is thin because he worked mostly from the phone that week.

What this does not prove: a push may be what sent him to the web tab. The hold tests exactly that. If the push was doing the work, the asks he does not open within 10 minutes get their push 10 minutes late, and the time-to-open guard below catches the slowdown.

The change

sm already drops a push whose ask was opened before the push came due (it marks it resolved). The change only sets the push's due time 10 minutes later when Rajesh is at the desk. One new table, push_desk_log, records each eligible push and whether he was at the desk when it was created and when it was sent, because measurement cannot read the push database.

Appendices

A. Behaviour

A.1 Eligible notices. owner_notices rows of kind message with blocking = 0, and of kind review_requested. Every other kind (approval_needed, board_*, follow notices, blocking messages) is never held and is not logged.

A.2 At the desk at time t. True when owner_events holds a row with surface = 'web', t − 300 000 ≤ at_ms < t and action not in the background list, and holds no row with surface = 'android' in the same interval. Background list: watch.state, restart.latest, session.models, model.status, board.badge, board.seen, doc.head, queue.stats, utilization.series, host.status, terminal.ticket, app.meta, push.register, follow.list, artifact.activity, artifact.list, session.obligations, review.status, handoff.defaults, doc.drafts, claim.list, notice.ack, app.bootstrap, review.policies. Keep the list in one constant shared with the metric queries' text (a test asserts the SQL files contain the same list). agent_prompt and terminal rows do not count: both can come from the phone.

A.3 Hold. When an eligible notice is created (create_notice, crates/sm-server/src/owner_push/notices.rs), and switches::is_on("autopm.desk-push-hold"), and the owner is at the desk at created_at: set notify_after = created_at + 600 s. Otherwise notify_after is unchanged from today. Delivery (deliver_notices_holding) is unchanged: at notify_after it closes the notice resolved if still_wanted is false or opened is true, else pushes. Overnight hold keeps its precedence. Repair-created notices (repair_notices) follow the same rule.

A.4 Push log. New table in the queue database (sm_send.db_path), the only database measurement can read for this:

CREATE TABLE push_desk_log (
  notice_id      TEXT PRIMARY KEY,   -- owner_notices.id
  kind           TEXT NOT NULL,      -- message | review_requested
  created_at_ms  INTEGER NOT NULL,
  desk_at_create INTEGER NOT NULL,   -- A.2 at created_at
  held           INTEGER NOT NULL,   -- 1 iff A.3 moved notify_after
  outcome        TEXT,               -- push | email | resolved | overnight | withdrawn; NULL until closed
  sent_at_ms     INTEGER,            -- set iff outcome is push
  desk_at_send   INTEGER             -- A.2 at sent_at_ms; set iff outcome is push
);

A row is inserted for every eligible notice at creation and updated once when the notice closes. It is written whether the switch is on or off, so the baseline accrues while the change is deployed dark; this additive table is the only difference with the switch off. Log write failures are logged and never block delivery. Rows older than 180 days are pruned with the activity log.

A.5 Off equals today. A test drives a fixture history (at-desk and away notices of every kind, opened and unopened) through creation and delivery with the switch off and asserts every notice's notify_after, outcome, and push and email calls equal those of the code on main. A second test with the switch on asserts A.3 for each case.

Z. FAQ

Why not hold until he leaves the desk? An ask he has not opened after 10 minutes may be one he has not seen; a bounded delay caps the cost of a wrong guess at 10 minutes.

Why not count typing to an agent as at the desk? The Claude app and the sm app terminal on the phone produce the same agent_prompt and terminal rows as the desk. Counting them inflated "at the desk" from 110 to 185 pushes and made week 2 look like he ignored the web.

Why log instead of reading the push database? Measurement attaches only the queue, activity, usage, utilization and bug databases. Attaching another one changes measurement rules, which needs Rajesh's yes.
