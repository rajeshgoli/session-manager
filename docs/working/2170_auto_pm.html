2170 — Auto PM: sm runs its own product manager

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 8bbcb31a · Ticket #2170 · 2026-10-15

Memo

Build it, in four tickets, and switch it on with a weekly allowance you can cancel. sm starts one standing product-manager agent on Opus 5.5. It studies what you actually do across the Android app, the web app, docs and your agent sessions. It proposes one change at a time, each with a success test fixed before the change ships. A Codex reviewer attacks each plan before anything is built. Then it ships the change behind a switch that turns it off in under a second, measures it, and keeps or reverts it. A change it cannot undo on its own, it builds and measures in a shadow copy of sm, then asks you a single yes or no. It records what worked and what did not in one file it keeps. Beyond that, it costs you no attention.

Three facts make this worth doing now:

  • You are sm's product manager, and sm learns nothing from watching you. It records which docs you open, when you answer agents and what you type into sessions, but no one reads that record to improve sm.
  • Reading your habits off stored state goes wrong. In one session I misread you twice. I took 136 messages never marked "handled" to mean you were behind; you had dealt with all of them. I took a quiet web inbox to mean the inbox was dead; you use it on Android as an alert for things you are expecting. A PM has to learn from behaviour, by surface, and test before acting.
  • Quota expires unused most weeks. Over the last eight weeks, Claude's weekly meter peaked between 39% and 92%; in a typical week more than 40 points expire. Codex peaked between 17% and 90%. A PM funded mostly from that surplus pays for itself from day 0.
Auto PM
One standing sm agent with the role autopm. It decides what to change in sm and proves whether the change helped. It writes plans and measures results; separate agents build each change.
Cycle
One week of auto PM work. sm opens a cycle ticket under goal #2170 every Monday, and the cycle closes when the auto PM publishes that week's readout.
Owner activity log
A new record, kept locally, of what you do in sm: which surface, which screen, which action, and whether you came from a push notification. It stores routes, ids and times, never message or prompt text.
Experiment
One change with a hypothesis, a metric, a baseline, a threshold fixed in advance, and a date by which it is measured.
Switch
An on/off setting that sm reads live, with no restart. Every behaviour change the auto PM ships sits behind one; off means exactly today's behaviour.
Revertable
sm can undo the change by itself with nothing lost: turn the switch off, or revert the commit and redeploy.
Shadow mode
Building a change on a branch and running it against a throwaway copy of sm's live state, without touching the real server or app, to gather evidence before asking you.
Playbook
The one file the auto PM keeps (docs/autopm/playbook.md): how you work, what worked, what did not, and its standing rules.
Points
Percentage points of a provider's weekly meter, the same number the sm app's usage meter shows. "20 Codex points" means 20% of a week of Codex.

How a change moves through the loop

The board carries all of it. #2170 is the goal and its lane is the autopm lane. Each Monday sm opens a cycle ticket under it and wakes the auto PM. The auto PM files each experiment's build tickets under the cycle ticket. sm auto-starts those tickets within budget, one fresh agent per ticket, in the normal workflow: PR, review, merge on a clean review, restart. The auto PM stays one seat for weeks. sm's default context handoff hands it to a fresh successor when its context fills, and its role, reminders and claims move with it.

How it knows a change helped

Every plan fixes four things before anything is built: the metric, as a query committed to the repo; its baseline over the previous 14 days; the bar to clear; and the measurement date. The default bar is a 25% improvement in the median, holding in both weeks of a 14-day window, while guard metrics do not get 25% worse. The guard metrics are your manual reverts, thumbs-down and bug reports about the area. With one user and noisy weeks, the bar is deliberately high: it detects big wins and ignores small ones. A change that misses the bar is reverted, even if it "might be helping".

Thumbs are optional, and silence counts as neutral. The app gets a small "What changed" card listing live changes with 👍 / 👎. 👎 reverts immediately.

The auto PM is measured too. Each readout reports changes kept against changes tried, points spent per kept change, and your thumbs. After four straight cycles with no kept change, sm pauses the auto PM and tells you once.

How it learns

The playbook is one Markdown file in git, capped at 300 lines, in five fixed sections: how you work (observed patterns, each with evidence and date), what worked, what did not, standing rules, and ranked open hypotheses. The auto PM reads it at the start of every cycle, and every handoff brief points its successor to it. It changes only through the cycle's readout PR, so you can read or edit it like any file. It starts from what this session learned:

  • You work from docs, not the inbox.
  • On Android, the inbox is an alert for things you are expecting.
  • Asks that need judgment on a long artifact stall, as #2114 in fractal-algo-rust shows.
  • Your attention is the scarcest currency.
  • Never infer disuse from stored state.

Two worked examples

Heavy asks. Hypothesis: asks that require reading a long artifact wait several times longer than yes/no asks. #2114 has waited since 2026-10-14 on a single three-way call about a day plan. Change: when an agent asks you to judge a doc, the doc reader opens with a decision card: the choices, the agent's recommendation in two lines, and one button each. Metric: median hours from ask to decision for judgment asks, from the activity log and doc review records. Bar: at least 25% faster across 14 days, with no rise in reversed decisions. Path: doc reader, behind a switch, no install. It ships alone.

Inbox alerts. Hypothesis: you open the Android inbox mostly within minutes of a push about something you asked for, and rarely otherwise. First the auto PM checks that this pattern exists in two weeks of the activity log. If it does, the change marks the pushes you are waiting on (replies to your own asks) as a separate, higher-priority class. Metric: minutes from push to open for those pushes, plus inbox opens with no push behind them as a guard. If the pattern is not there, the playbook records that and nothing ships.

Budget

Each provider's allowance is a dedicated amount plus surplus. The surplus is quota the projection says will expire, released only from day four of the week and only beyond a 20-point safety margin. sm counts the auto PM's spend across its own seat and successors, the agents building its tickets, and the reviews of their PRs. At zero, sm stops starting autopm tickets; agents mid-ticket reach a safe stopping point and stop. "Skip this week" in Settings sets that week's allowance to zero. The auto PM's own thinking runs on Claude. Building prefers Codex, which has the most unused quota.

What it costs you

  • Once: reviewing this memo, switching it on in Settings, and installing one app update.
  • Weekly, optional: a one-page readout published as a doc, with no notification. You see it among your docs if you look.
  • At most one message a week, and only when a shadow-built change needs your yes or no. It holds up to three decisions, each answerable in one word. Unanswered after 14 days counts as no.

A. Owner activity log

A.1 Table. owner_events in the queue database (sm_send.db_path, the database holding owner_messages).

CREATE TABLE owner_events (
  id          INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,
  at_ms       INTEGER NOT NULL,          -- server receive time, unix ms
  surface     TEXT NOT NULL,             -- android | web | agent_prompt | terminal
  device_id   TEXT,                      -- mobile_device_enrollments.device_id, if known
  action      TEXT NOT NULL,             -- see A.3
  target_kind TEXT,                      -- session|doc|ticket|pr|thread|notice|setting|queue_job|experiment|lane
  target_id   TEXT,
  screen      TEXT,                      -- x-sm-screen header, else NULL
  nav         TEXT,                      -- push | link | nav | launch | NULL (x-sm-nav)
  notice_id   TEXT,                      -- push notice id when nav = push
  status      INTEGER,                   -- HTTP status; NULL for agent_prompt/terminal
  duration_ms INTEGER                    -- handler time; NULL where not applicable
);
CREATE INDEX owner_events_at ON owner_events(at_ms);
CREATE INDEX owner_events_action ON owner_events(action, at_ms);

A.2 Surface.

  • android: the request passed device_auth_user (http.rs:16135) or arrived through the MobileApp Access application (mark_in_app, http.rs:2313). Owner pages loaded in the app's WebView (DocReader.kt:69) count as android.
  • web: the request passed owner_web_guard (http.rs:15841) and not the above.
  • agent_prompt: a Claude UserPromptSubmit hook or a Codex prompt the server classes as owner-typed (is_owner_typed_prompt, owner_messages.rs:131; Codex path sessions.rs:8529). target_kind=session, target_id = the session. The Claude app, claude.ai and the terminal are not distinguishable; record them all as agent_prompt.
  • terminal: input on the app terminal websocket (http.rs:9680). Coalesce to one event per session per 60 s of input.

Requests from agents (an x-sm-session-id header or the local-agent gateway) and loopback requests without owner auth are never recorded.

A.3 Action. action is a stable name from a static map keyed by method and route template, for example GET /client/sessions/{id} → session.open, GET /docs/{id} → doc.open, POST /inbox/…/reply → inbox.reply, PUT /client/board/order → board.order. A route absent from the map records its template string, such as GET /client/queue, so nothing is silently dropped. Keep the map in one module and cover it with a test that every owner route resolves to some action. Ids from path parameters fill target_id. Query strings and bodies are never stored.

A.4 Background requests are not events. A request is background when it carries x-sm-bg: 1, or when no client header is present and its route is on the poll list: /watch/state, /client/host-status, /client/board/badge, /client/utilization/series, /client/usage/meters, /client/queue/stats, /inbox when format=json and x-sm-bg is set. Background requests are not stored.

A.5 Client headers (ticket 4). Android adds x-sm-screen (the current screen's route name, for example board, session/detail, inbox/thread, doc) and x-sm-bg in its OkHttp interceptor (AuthInterceptor.kt). It sends x-sm-nav: push:<notice_id> on the first request after a notification tap, launch after a cold start, and nav otherwise. The web client sends the same headers from its router (crates/sm-server/src/web/ui.js).

A.6 Retention. Rows older than 180 days are deleted by a daily prune. The log is never sent off the machine.

A.7 Read. GET /autopm/events?since_ms=&until_ms=&surface=&action=&limit= (owner or autopm role; limit ≤ 10 000, ordered by at_ms, cursor by id). CLI: sm autopm events [--since 7d] [--surface S] [--action A] [--json].

A.8 Example. You tap a push about a reply on Android, read the thread, then open the doc it links:

B. Switches and experiments

B.1 Switches.

CREATE TABLE experiment_switches (
  key          TEXT PRIMARY KEY,   -- e.g. "decision-card"
  on_          INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  changed_at_ms INTEGER NOT NULL,
  changed_by   TEXT NOT NULL,      -- autopm | owner | guard | feedback | killswitch
  reason       TEXT
);
  • The server holds an in-memory copy, updated on every write, and code asks switches::is_on("key"). A missing key reads as off.
  • When autopm.enabled is false (C.1), every switch reads as off whatever its row says.
  • Clients receive switches: {key: bool} in /client/bootstrap and /client/settings; the web client re-reads on its existing stale-build check, and Android on resume.
  • Writes: PUT /autopm/switches/{key} {on, reason} (owner or autopm role).
  • Off must equal pre-change behaviour exactly. Every experiment's build PR includes a test asserting that.

B.2 Experiment record.

CREATE TABLE autopm_experiments (

  cycle_ticket   INTEGER NOT NULL,
  title          TEXT NOT NULL,
  hypothesis     TEXT NOT NULL,
  path           TEXT NOT NULL,      -- switch | revert | shadow
  switch_key     TEXT,               -- required when path = switch
  tickets_json   TEXT NOT NULL,      -- build ticket numbers
  metric_query   TEXT NOT NULL,      -- repo path docs/autopm/metrics/<id>.sql
  guard_queries  TEXT NOT NULL,      -- JSON array of repo paths
  baseline       REAL,
  bar            TEXT NOT NULL,      -- e.g. "median -25% both weeks"
  window_days    INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 14,
  live_at_ms     INTEGER,
  measure_at_ms  INTEGER,
  result         REAL,

);

B.3 States. planned → building → dark → live → measuring → kept | reverted; shadow experiments go planned → building → shadowed → awaiting_owner → (yes) building → dark → … | (no or 14 days) abandoned. Any state may go to abandoned with a note. dark means merged and deployed with its switch off. live means switched on. sm enforces at most autopm.max_live (default 3) experiments in live or measuring. Routes: POST /autopm/experiments, PATCH /autopm/experiments/{id}, GET /autopm/experiments; CLI sm autopm exp {new|set|list|show}.

B.4 Health guard. Every 60 s the server samples three counters over the trailing 10 minutes: owner-route responses with status ≥ 500, failed sm send deliveries, and caught handler panics. If any counter exceeds max(3, twice its 95th percentile over the previous 7 days), the guard does two things. It switches off every switch turned on within the last 60 minutes, with changed_by=guard. Then it wakes the auto PM with [sm autopm] guard reverted <keys>: <counter> <value>. The guard never touches switches older than 60 minutes; those failures are the auto PM's to diagnose.

B.5 Kill switch. autopm.enabled=false (Settings › Auto PM, or PUT /client/settings) takes effect immediately:

  1. All switches read off (B.1).
  2. sm stops auto-starting autopm-lane tickets.
  3. Cycle creation stops.
  4. The auto PM and every agent on an autopm ticket get [sm autopm] disabled by Rajesh: push work in progress, comment on your ticket, stop.

Re-enabling restores the rows' own switch values.

B.6 Revert path. For path=revert changes: git revert the merge commit, open a PR, wait for a clean review, merge, then scripts/restart-rust-server.sh. If main has moved on in a way that conflicts, deploy the previous build with --allow-behind-main first, then land the revert.

C.2 Allowance per provider per weekly window (Claude weekly_all; Codex codex_10080 of the account the build agents use):

allowance = 0                               if skip_window_starting = this window's start
          = dedicated                       before 96 h into the window
          = dedicated + min(cap, max(0, headroom - margin))   from 96 h on
headroom  = projected_free_headroom_points from build_projection (usage_report.rs:851)

Example: on Thursday the Claude projection says the week ends at 55%, so headroom is 45. The allowance is 5 + min(15, 45 − 20) = 20 points.

C.3 Spend. Spend is the token use in usage.db seat_tokens for the current window, converted to points with the fitted rates in quota_rates.rs. It covers three groups of seats:

  1. Every seat that has held the autopm role, including handoff successors.
  2. Every seat that has claimed a ticket under #2170.
  3. Review runs on PRs linked to those tickets.

The PM's in-context research helpers count as part of its seat. GET /autopm/budget returns {window_start, resets_at, providers: {claude: {allowance, spent, remaining}, codex: {…}}}; CLI sm autopm budget.

C.4 Enforcement. sm does not auto-start an autopm-lane ticket whose resolved provider has remaining ≤ 0. When a provider first reaches zero in a window, sm sends the auto PM and every agent on an autopm ticket of that provider [sm autopm] <provider> budget spent: reach a safe stop. A safe stop means: push the work, comment on the ticket what remains, then go idle. Overshoot is bounded by the work already in flight. The auto PM itself checks sm autopm budget before spawning any helper, and stops researching when its provider is at zero.

D. The autopm role, cycles and the board

D.1 Role. autopm is an sm role like maintainer (agent registrations). sm allows one live holder. Handoff moves roles to the successor (specs/1651_context_handoff.html, appendix G). Only a seat sm started as the auto PM may register it.

D.2 Starting the seat. When autopm.enabled becomes true and no seat holds the role, sm ensures four things:

  1. #2170 is a lane. If it is not, sm adds it at the bottom with added_by=sm:autopm-enable.
  2. The lane-scope review policy for that lane is set to Codex gpt-6-sol, medium effort, with the usual fallback chain (review/mod.rs:48). Your act of enabling the auto PM stands as the owner's choice of reviewer.
  3. The current cycle ticket exists (D.3).
  4. An auto-start row exists on the cycle ticket with autopm.agent and the brief from docs/autopm/brief.md (appendix H).

The started seat registers the role first thing.

D.3 Cycles. Every Monday at 09:07 in your timezone, sm creates the issue Auto PM cycle <ISO year>-W<week> in session-manager as a sub-issue of #2170, with label autopm. The body template is goals: pick or continue up to three experiments; measure those due; publish the readout. sm then wakes the role holder with [sm autopm] cycle <week> opened: #<N>. If last week's cycle ticket is still open, sm closes it with the comment "rolled over to #N". The auto PM closes a cycle ticket by publishing its readout (G.2). No cycle is created in a skipped window.

D.5 Build tickets. One agent per ticket, tier from the ticket's Tier: line (owner_settings.rs:531). The auto PM writes Tier: Mid and names Codex (gpt-6-sol medium or gpt-5.6-terra high) in the auto-start unless Codex has no budget left. Build agents follow CLAUDE.md's workflow unchanged: worktree, PR with Closes #N, sm request-review, merge on a clean review, restart with the script, clean up. The ticket body states the experiment id, the switch key, the off-equals-today test, and the metric query path.

D.6 Collisions. Before filing a build ticket, the auto PM lists open session-manager claims and Rajesh's open session-manager tickets. If the files it expects to touch overlap any PR from those claims, it files the ticket sm board after the conflicting ticket. It never works in an area one of Rajesh's open tickets addresses; it records the idea in the playbook's hypotheses instead.

D.7 Context. The auto PM uses the default Claude handoff policy (threshold 35%, handoff/policy.rs:29). Its handoff note always lists live experiments with their measurement dates, the cycle ticket, and the path of the playbook. Measurement dates are set as sm remind reminders, which handoff moves to the successor.

E. Shadow mode and asks

E.1 Shadow build. The build agent works on a branch and runs the change on a scratch server: a copy of live state with rust_core.runtime_enabled: false (the scratch-server recipe used for local benches), on a port other than 8420. Evidence comes from one of two sources:

  • replaying the last 14 days of owner_events against both builds;
  • computing the metric offline on the copy.

The agent records the evidence in decision_note and sets state awaiting_owner. A shadow build never touches the live server, the live databases or the installed app.

E.2 The ask. POST /autopm/ask sends one owner message, non-blocking, title Auto PM: N decisions. Each decision is at most 120 words: what it changes, the evidence, what yes does, what no does. You answer in the inbox thread, one word per decision ("1 yes, 2 no"). sm parses the reply into each experiment's state, and the auto PM asks again in plain words only if it cannot parse the reply. A decision unanswered after 14 days becomes abandoned, with note "no answer". The auto PM does not ask about it again unless the evidence changes materially.

F. Measurement and feedback

F.1 Plan contents. Each experiment's plan is a short HTML doc in docs/autopm/plans/<id>.html, opened as a PR and reviewed with sm request-review (Codex per D.2). It must state:

  • the hypothesis;
  • the evidence from the activity log;
  • the change;
  • its path (switch, revert or shadow) and why;
  • the metric query and the guard queries;
  • the baseline value, the bar and the window;
  • the points estimate;
  • the build tickets.

Converged means the review raised no P1, or the auto PM answered each P1 per CLAUDE.md's review loop.

F.2 Decision rule (default, overridable only in the plan, before building):

  • keep when the metric beats the bar in both 7-day halves of the window, every guard is within 25% of its baseline, and there are no 👎;
  • reverted immediately on any 👎 (the server switches it off, changed_by=feedback), or at measurement when the bar is missed or a guard fails;
  • extend once by 14 days when the metric has fewer than 10 observations; still under 10 after that means reverted with note "insufficient data".

A kept change keeps its switch for 30 days, then a cleanup ticket removes the switch and the old code path.

F.3 Feedback. A "What changed" card appears in Settings › Auto PM (Android and web), and as a dismissible chip on any screen a live switch changes. It lists live experiments: title, one line, live since, 👍 / 👎. POST /client/autopm/feedback {experiment_id, vote: "up"|"down", note?} (owner only) increments the counts. A down vote also switches the experiment off and wakes the auto PM.

F.4 The auto PM's own score, in every readout: experiments kept / finished, points spent per kept experiment, votes, changes you reverted or switched off yourself. After four consecutive closed cycles with zero kept, sm sets autopm.enabled=false and sends one owner message: "Auto PM paused: four cycles, nothing kept. Readouts: …".

Z. FAQ

Why one standing seat instead of a fresh agent per cycle? Rajesh's call. The playbook and the handoff note carry continuity; one seat lets reminders and switches have a single owner.

Why not let research run on a cheap tier and judgment on the top tier, as separate seats? Rajesh rejected the split. The auto PM is one judgment seat, and research helpers live in its context.

Why are restart and deploy scripts ask-only when a commit revert would undo them? Undoing a change goes through those scripts. A broken one can block its own revert.

Why does Analytics › Time not suffice? It attributes agent time, including time spent waiting on "you". It records nothing about what Rajesh does on each surface, which is the auto PM's main input.

Can it tell the Claude app from the terminal? No. The prompt hook carries no origin. Both are agent_prompt.

What about Codex accounts other than the build account? Allowance applies to the account the build agents resolve to. Others are untouched.
